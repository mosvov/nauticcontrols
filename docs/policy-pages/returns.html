Limited warranty

Unless a product page states otherwise, products are covered by a 2-year limited warranty from the ship date to the original purchaser. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

The warranty does not cover:

  • Damage from improper installation, wiring, or power supply
  • Water ingress from improper enclosure sealing or cable glands
  • Modifications, reverse engineering, or unauthorized repairs
  • Firmware, software, or configuration issues on developer boards and DIY kits
  • Normal wear, cosmetic damage, or misuse
  • Products purchased from unauthorized sellers

Developer boards and DIY kits (including Signal K Gateway Kit and Engine Monitoring Kit) are sold as development-style hardware. They are not drop-in finished consumer gateways. Warranty covers the hardware as delivered, not application software you flash or assemble.

How to make a claim

  1. Email us with your order number, SKU, a short description of the issue, and photos if relevant.
  2. We will confirm whether an RMA is needed. In many cases we can replace the unit with a future shipment or credit without a physical return.
  3. Do not ship products back until we authorize a return and provide instructions.

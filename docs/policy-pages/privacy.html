This Privacy Policy describes how Nautic Controls (operated by Rigsense LLC, Florida, USA) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit www.nauticcontrols.com or place an order.

Questions: info@nauticcontrols.com.

Information we collect

  • Order and account details you provide at Shopify Checkout (name, email, shipping address, billing address, phone if supplied).
  • Payment information processed by Shopify Payments or other Shopify-supported processors. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Contact messages you send to info@nauticcontrols.com.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, collected through standard web logs and analytics tools on the storefront and Shopify Admin (see below).

How we use information

  • Fulfill orders, ship products, and provide customer support
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping updates, warranty replies)
  • Improve the storefront, measure marketing performance, prevent fraud, and meet legal or tax obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money. We use advertising measurement tools (Meta Pixel and Google Analytics) that may be treated as a "share" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some US state privacy laws. California residents can opt out via our Do Not Sell or Share page or by emailing info@nauticcontrols.com.

Sharing

We share data with service providers who help us run the store, including:

  • Shopify (storefront, checkout, payments, order management, and Shopify analytics)
  • Vercel (hosting and Web Analytics / Speed Insights for the storefront)
  • Google Analytics (site and ecommerce event measurement)
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel (ads measurement and retargeting events)
  • Carriers used to deliver packages (name, address, and contact details needed for shipping)
  • Email providers that deliver transactional or support messages

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.

Cookies and similar technologies

Shopify Checkout and the storefront may use cookies or similar technologies for session, cart, security, and analytics purposes (including Shopify session cookies, Vercel Analytics, Google Analytics, and the Meta Pixel). You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect checkout, cart, or measurement.

Retention

We keep order and support records as long as needed for fulfillment, warranty, accounting, and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

Email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal retention needs (for example, completed orders and tax records). For data held in Shopify Checkout or your customer account, you may also use Shopify account tools where available.

Children

This store is intended for adults purchasing marine electronics and related hardware. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

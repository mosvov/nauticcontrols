Nautic Controls ships Hat Labs hardware from Florida, USA for domestic US customers.

Processing

Orders typically leave our Florida location within 1-2 business days after payment clears, subject to stock. You will receive tracking when the carrier scans the package.

Delivery

We fulfill via common US carriers (USPS / UPS / similar). Transit times depend on destination and the rate selected at checkout. Final shipping cost and tax are calculated in Shopify Checkout.

What we ship

  • In-stock boards, accessories, and kits held in Florida
  • Packaging suitable for electronics; kits may ship as component sets until assembled kit photography is available

International

This storefront is focused on US domestic fulfillment. International orders may be declined or require a custom quote. Customers outside the US can also purchase directly from shop.hatlabs.fi.

Damaged or lost shipments

If a package arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and contents and contact us within 7 days. We will arrange a replacement or refund for carrier-damaged goods we confirm.

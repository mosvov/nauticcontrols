Rates

Rates apply at Shopify Checkout based on merchandise subtotal before tax. We buy discounted carrier labels through Shopify Shipping when fulfilling (USPS / UPS / similar). Transit time depends on destination; tracking is emailed when the carrier scans the package.

Processing

Orders typically ship within 1-2 business days after payment clears, subject to stock.

What we ship

  • In-stock boards, accessories, and kits
  • Packaging suitable for electronics; kits may ship as component sets until assembled kit photography is available

Other destinations

Need shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, Canada, Mexico, or elsewhere? Contact us for a quote.

Damaged or lost shipments

If a package arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and contents and contact us within 7 days. We will arrange a replacement or refund for carrier-damaged goods we confirm.

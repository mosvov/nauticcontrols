Nautic Controls is a Florida-based US stocking reseller of Hat Labs Oy open marine electronics for boaters, DIY installers, and Signal K builders.

What we sell

  • Hat Labs development boards (SH-ESP32, HALMET, SH-RPi) and HALPI2 marine computers
  • Enclosures and NMEA 2000 / SP13 connectors
  • US-oriented DIY kits (board + enclosure + connectors) with install guidance from Nautic Controls

Hardware is designed and manufactured by Hat Labs Oy (Finland). We are a non-exclusive US stocking reseller and importer of record.

How we differ from Hat Labs direct

  • Ships from the USA
  • USD checkout and US customer support as first line
  • Kit bundles and US-focused product copy (technical docs remain on Hat Labs)

A NMEA 2000 compatible Micro-C style panel connector, with pigtail wires - no soldering required! This connector is useful when you want to place a product such as HALMET, SH-ESP32 or SH-RPi in an enclosure with easy-to-use NMEA 2000 connector.

As opposed to the previous variant, this one only requires a 12 mm hole and is more compact in size.

This is how you would normally use the connector:

  1. Drill a 12 mm hole in an enclosure (conical drill bits are great for this)
  2. Optionally, cut the pigtails to length
  3. Strip the wire ends
  4. Screw the wires on a terminal block connector, following the pinout on the PCB:
    • Black to GND
    • Red to 12V
    • White to H
    • Blue to L
  5. Insert the connector into the hole and tighten the nut.

The connector is rear-mount (panel nut outside the enclosure). This makes it easier to achieve a watertight seal, and fitting a spanner is straightforward.

Specifications:

    • Ingress Protection: IP67
    • Pigtail wire length: 30 cm (12 in)
    • Panel hole diameter: 12 mm
    • Wire gauges:
      • Black and red: 22 AWG
      • White and Blue: 24 AWG

A waterproof enclosure with 5 pre-drilled 13 mm holes and 3 PG7 blind plugs. This is the perfect, easy-to-use enclosure for your microcontroller projects. No drilling required!

 The holes fit NMEA 2000 panel connectors, SP13 connectors or PG7 cable glands. The holes on the long edge can also be enlarged with a step drill bit to accept larger connector sizes.

 IMPORTANT: The SH-ESP32 does NOT fit in this enclosure if any of the holes on the long edge have a blind plug installed. Or in other words: when used with an SH-ESP32, all three holes on the long edge must have SP13 or NMEA 2000 panel connectors installed, and even then, it's a tight fit.

 Specifications:

 
- External dimensions: 100*68*50 mm (length including the wall mount lips 132 mm)

- Ingress Protection level: IP65

- Material: ABS

- Stainless steel (A4 / AIS316) screws

- PCB mounting screws included

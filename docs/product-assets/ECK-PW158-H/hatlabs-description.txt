A waterproof enclosure with a transparent lid and 6 pre-drilled 13 mm holes plus 4 PG7 blind plugs. A snug fit for Raspberry Pis or a spacious home for microcontroller boards - no drilling required!

 The holes fit NMEA 2000 panel connectors, SP13 connectors or PG7 cable glands. They can also be enlarged with a step drill bit to accept larger connector sizes.

 
- External dimensions: 158x90x60 mm (6.22x3.54x2.36 inches)

- Weight 186 g (6.56 oz)

- Body material ABS, lid transparent polycarbonate

- Functional beige color

- IP65 ingress protection rating

- Stainless steel (A4/AISI 316) screws for added durability

- Accommodates Raspberry Pi and 2 HATs

- When used with a Raspberry Pi, a fan and a Raspberry Pi heat sink are recommended

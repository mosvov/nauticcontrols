A five-pin SP13 connector with a female cable plug and a male panel connector. Suitable to be used for example as an NMEA 0183 or other data connector. The panel connector has a pigtail for easier and faster installation: just trim the wires to the correct length, strip and connect to a screw terminal!


Contents:

  1. One pigtail panel connector with a back nut (male)
  2. One cable plug (female)


Specifications:

  1. Ingress Protection rating: IP68
  2. Contact diameter: 5×1.0 mm
  3. Rated current: 5 A
  4. Cable range: 4-6.5 mm
  5. Pigtail wire length: 20 cm
  6. Pigtail wire gauge: 22 AWG (0.34 mm2)

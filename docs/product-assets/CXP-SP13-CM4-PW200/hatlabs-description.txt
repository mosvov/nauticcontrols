A four-pin SP13 connector with a male cable plug and a female panel connector. Suitable to be used for example as a sensor data connector. The panel connector has a pigtail for easier and faster installation: just trim the wires to the correct length, strip and connect to a screw terminal!

 

 Contents:

 
- One pigtail panel connector with a back nut (male)

- One cable plug (female)

 
 

 Specifications:

 
- Ingress Protection rating: IP68

- Contact diameter: 4×1.0 mm

- Rated current: 5 A

- Cable range: 4-6.5 mm

- Pigtail wire length: 20 cm

- Pigtail wire gauge: 22 AWG (0.34 mm2)

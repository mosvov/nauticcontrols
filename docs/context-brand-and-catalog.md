# Brand and catalog (public positioning)

**Updated:** 2026-10-09

## Brand

**Nautic Controls** is a US marine and RV electronics reseller: boards, kits, NMEA 2000 / Signal K gear, and accessories.

Public promise:

- US stock
- Ships from the US
- Free ground shipping on orders $75+ (lower 48)
- Clear product docs and email support

Do not present the store as an exclusive stockist or “home” for any single manufacturer.

## Manufacturers

Product pages may name the manufacturer brand (for example Hat Labs on a Hat Labs board). That is product attribution, not store identity. The catalog will include multiple brands over time.

## Category IA

| Collection | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Kits | Install bundles (board + enclosure + connectors when offered) |
| Development Boards | Bare boards and modules |
| Marine Computers | HALPI2 and similar hosts |
| Accessories | Enclosures, connectors, small parts |
| NMEA 2000 | Backbone and network parts |

RV is an audience and use case, not a separate invented brand.

## Homepage

HALPI2 remains the single flagship hero. Surrounding chrome is multi-brand marine/RV, not manufacturer-exclusive.

## PDP vendor attribution

- Shopify `vendor` = manufacturer brand for that SKU
- Titles can include the manufacturer name when it helps search
- Docs links point to the manufacturer’s docs when that is the source of truth
- Shipping copy: “US stock” / “Ships from the US” only (no warehouse location, no sourcing story)

## Public copy rules

Allowed: Ships from the US · US stock · Free shipping $75+ · lower 48 · manufacturer brand on a PDP · marine and RV electronics

Forbidden on customer-facing surfaces: warehouse city, dropship, partner warehouses, EU vs US sourcing, exclusive-reseller framing, “Hat Labs US stockist”

Internal supplier, PO, and sourcing notes live in separate docs (for example `context-hat-labs-partnership.md`). Do not copy those into the storefront or policy pages.
